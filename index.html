📋 What is the PSI?

  • One visit within 5 business days before you get the keys (possession). [1]
  • On a business day, between 9am and 4pm. You can bring 2 people. [1]
  • The aim: the house is in the same condition as when you signed, and agreed work is done. It's not for raising new minor faults. [2][3]
  • Found something the seller must fix? Tell them, and you get one more visit to re-check before possession. [1]

🎒 Bring

  • The contract (Offer & Acceptance + special conditions)
  • List of inclusions / exclusions
  • Agreed repair list
  • Phone (charged) for photos

⚠️ Found a problem?

  1. Photo it (wide shot + close-up).
  2. Write a short note: what, where.
  3. Tell your settlement agent / conveyancer straight away, before settlement.

If the condition has changed since you signed, you may be able to claim compensation from the seller. [5]

General information, not legal advice. Check your own contract with your settlement agent or lawyer.

WA law = a legal requirement for the seller before the title transfers.
